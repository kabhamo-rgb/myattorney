Double Collection or Collection After the Debt Is Paid — How to Get a Refund

One of the most common over-collection situations: the debt has already been paid — in full or in part — and collection continues; or you were charged twice for the same debt. In such cases you may be entitled to a refund.

In brief: if an amount higher than the actual debt was collected from you — for example collection after payment, double billing, or incorrect interest — the main procedure for recovery is a claim of payment ("pareti") under Section 19 of the Enforcement and Collection Law.

When does over-collection occur?

  • Collection after the debt is paid — you paid the debt but the file was not closed and collection continues.
  • Double billing — the same debt is collected twice (for example a direct payment to the creditor + collection in the file).
  • Payment not credited — a payment you made was not updated in the file balance.
  • Incorrect interest and linkage — interest calculated beyond what is permitted inflates the debt.

What is a claim of payment ("pareti")?

A claim of payment ("pareti") (Section 19 of the Enforcement and Collection Law, 5727-1967) is a request in which the debtor shows that the debt was paid — in whole or in part — and asks to stop collection and recover amounts collected beyond the debt. The request is submitted to the Enforcement and Collection Authority registrar, and sometimes a hearing is held.

How do you get a refund? Step by step

  1. Gather supporting documents: receipts, payment confirmations, and the Enforcement and Collection Authority file printout.
  2. Check the original debt balance against all amounts actually collected (including interest and expenses).
  3. File a claim of payment ("pareti") or another appropriate request to recover the over-collected amount.
  4. As needed — conduct a hearing with the creditor and the Enforcement and Collection Authority registrar until recovery.
